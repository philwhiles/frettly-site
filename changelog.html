Changelog

New features, improvements and fixes in Frettler, with the newest release first.

Version 1.4.0

9 October 2026 Latest release

More ways to organise and navigate your workspace and favour preferred chord voicings, with multiple usability enhancements.

Workspace and navigation

  • Collapsible panels. Collapse fretboards and chord organisers to keep larger documents easy to scan and rearrange. Their contents remain available when you reopen them.
  • Keyboard panel navigation. Navigate between panels using the keyboard, with shortcuts for moving panels and opening or collapsing them.

Chords and voicings

  • Favourite voicings. Save a preferred voicing for a chord, instrument and tuning. Frettler uses an available favourite when you create a chord diagram or change its chord, including diagrams created from the Scale Harmony sheet.
  • Chord inversions. Chord organiser diagrams identify inversions, with slash-chord titles showing the bass note and an information popover explaining the inversion.
  • Voicing preferences. New Chords settings let you include or exclude inversions and voicings with omitted chord notes. A saved favourite remains stored if these settings make it unavailable.
  • Reverse strumming. Hold Option while clicking a chord diagram to reverse the direction of its strum.

Improvements and fixes

  • Fixed print scaling so the selected scale is applied correctly.
  • Fixed portrait printing to use a layout suited to the page orientation.
  • Corrected banjo chord diagrams to respect the shortened fifth string.
  • Further improvements to chord voicing calculations.
